## Related installation checks (same day)

- Time zones: HA `Europe/London`; Predbat rates carry explicit offsets; the Solis inverter clock
  (`sensor.solis_rtc`) runs on UK local time. The daily 03:00 RTC sync automation (`button.solis_sync_rtc`)
  had been disabled since Nov 2025; re-enabled and verified (offset +28 s → −3 s). Without it the
  inverter would stay on BST after 25 Oct and its timed charge window would be one hour early.
- Predbat warning "Unable to load the load forecast from predheat.heat_energy" while space heating is off:
  see `docs/predheat_calibration.md` (template sensor that falls back to a zero forecast).
